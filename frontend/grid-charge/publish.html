The platform

Not a charger dashboard. An energy intelligence platform.

Instead of letting every EV charge on its own and stack up a large peak, Grid Charge weighs building demand, grid capacity, battery SOC, departure times and solar availability — then decides how much power each vehicle gets.

Step 1ML Predicts

Forecasts building demand, solar output and grid congestion six hours ahead.

Step 2Optimization Decides

Scores every vehicle by urgency, SOC and grid slack, then allocates power every 15 minutes.

Step 3Controller Acts

Sends set-points to each charger and keeps the site inside its grid limit.

Step 4Dashboard Explains

Shows every decision and the reason behind it, in plain language.
